Interview problem

Design and implement an in-memory caching library that applications can use to store and retrieve key-value pairs.

The cache should support configurable capacity and eviction policies.

Your implementation should be extensible enough to introduce new eviction strategies without rewriting the core cache.

Reqauirements

1	Which eviction policies?	LRU and FIFO, configurable when creating the cache.
2	Concurrent access?	Yes. Multiple threads may call cache operations concurrently.
3	What happens when a key is updated?	Replace its value and reset its TTL. It must not increase the number of entries.
4	Expired but never accessed?	Lazy expiration is acceptable. Expired entries must not be returned, and capacity reclamation must account for expiration.
5	Expected complexity?	Target O(1) average get, put, and remove, excluding expiration cleanup.
6	Key/value types?	Generic Cache<K,V>. Keys and values must be non-null. Keys require stable equals() and hashCode().
7	Retention period?	Optional TTL per entry. Without TTL, entries remain until eviction, replacement, or removal.
8	Does access refresh TTL?	get() does not. put() resets TTL using the newly supplied TTL.
9	Supported operations?	get, put, remove, and size.
10	Cache reaches capacity?	Reclaim expired entries first where practical; if still full, evict according to the configured policy.
11	Capacity configuration?	Fixed positive capacity specified at construction. No dynamic resizing required.
